Notes

  • The convent(s) in charge of the day shall:
    • Organize the liturgy: lead the Liturgy of the Hours (including Exposition and the Holy Rosary).
    • Introduce the resource speaker(s).
    • Moderate the sessions and open forum (if any).
    • Deliver the word of thanks (including tokens).
  • The Studentate Liturgical Committee (SLC) takes charge of the liturgical materials and vestments.
  • Sacramental Confession is encouraged among the brothers.
  • Two UST buses will be available at Santo Domingo Church for brothers coming from abroad and from non-Luzon communities.
  • Do not forget your medicines.
  • Update your iBreviary app.
